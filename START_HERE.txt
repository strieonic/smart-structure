Test 1: Register as Expert (2 minutes)
---------------------------------------
1. Open frontend/index.html
2. Click "Expert Account" card
3. Click "Expert Register" tab
4. Fill Step 1: Basic Information
   - Name: Er. John Doe
   - Email: john@example.com
   - Password: Test@12345
   - Phone: +91 9876543210
   Click "Next"

5. Fill Step 2: Professional Details
   - Type: Structural Engineer
   - Experience: 10-15 Years
   - Location: Mumbai
   - License: MH/SE/2024/001
   Click "Next"

6. Fill Step 3: Specializations
   - Select: Seismic Design, Foundation Design
   - Summary: "Experienced structural engineer..."
   Click "Register as Expert"

7. You'll be auto-logged in and redirected to Expert Dashboard!


Test 2: View Expert Dashboard (1 minute)
-----------------------------------------
After registration, you'll see:
- Total Pending Queries
- Total Solved Queries
- Average Rating
- Profile Views
- Recent queries list


Test 3: Post a Query as User (1 minute)
----------------------------------------
1. Logout (top right)
2. Register/Login as regular user
3. Navigate to "Professional Network"
4. Click "Post Query"
5. Fill query details:
   - Title: "Foundation Design Review"
   - Description: "Need expert review..."
   - Category: "foundation"
6. Submit


Test 4: Respond as Expert (1 minute)
-------------------------------------
1. Logout and login as expert
2. Go to Expert Dashboard
3. See the new query in pending list
4. Click "Accept Query"
5. Click "Respond"
6. Enter your expert response
7. Submit

Done! You've completed the full workflow! 🎉
